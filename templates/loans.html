When you apply, you will enter your monthly salary on the application form. Finova checks that salary against the selected loan's minimum salary and salary-based loan limit, and also checks your active card's monthly limit and current-month usage. The entered salary is saved with the application as a snapshot. These are configurable demo rules, not a real-world lending decision.
